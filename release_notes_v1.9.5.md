# AniDash 1.9.5

Developer: Anshdeep Singh  
GitHub: anshdeepofficial

## Playback and player improvements

- Improved stream startup, fallback handling, buffering feedback, and playback recovery.
- Added automatic SUB fallback and clear SUB/DUB availability information.
- Replaced overlapping loaders with one consistent startup, buffering, and seeking indicator.
- Fixed repeated double-tap seeking so taps accumulate before one smooth seek.
- Fixed manual pause being overridden by automatic recovery.
- Unified controls: lock at right-center, fullscreen at bottom, and episodes at the top.
- Fixed landscape lock so it preserves the phone's current side and slightly improved rotation responsiveness.
- Improved auto-skip, filler highlighting, resume progress, and Continue Watching behavior.
- Added available-language information to anime and movie About pages.
- Improved exact movie matching and alternate-server fallback.
