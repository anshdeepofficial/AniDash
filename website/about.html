About

One place for your anime routine.

AniDash is an open-source anime companion focused on a clean, connected experience across discovery, playback, progress, downloads and manga.

Why AniDash exists

The goal is to reduce the number of separate tools needed to discover a title, understand it, watch it, remember progress and manage a personal library.

Open source

The source is public, issues can be reported on GitHub, and focused contributions can be reviewed through the project repository.
